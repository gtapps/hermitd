# Task policy

## Record threshold
Record work that outlasts the turn or needs a human. Plain questions open nothing.

## Confirmation and cancel words
Use “ok”, “yes”, or “confirmed” to confirm a posted result at its current revision. Use “cancel” or “stop this task” to cancel with a reason. A pending micro-proposal takes precedence. When every PR a task produced is merged, the task is done: close it without waiting for a separate confirmation.

## Card, notice and standup wording
Keep cards concise: requester, definition of done, due date and outcome. State the queue position when accepting work for later. Show handles when two or more tasks are open in the conversation; in DMs also require tasks.handle_in_dm. A stall notice names the status and next step. Standup groups promised, late and waiting work by person.

## Questions inside a task's place
Do not log questions unless they change the task.

## Bookkeeping ledger duty
Routine bookkeeping one-liners belong in the ledger duty, not individual task records.
